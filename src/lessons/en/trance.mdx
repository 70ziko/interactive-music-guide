Trance is the most **melodic** of the club genres: a rolling bassline, a 16th-note arpeggio, a gated pad, and a long breakdown where the whole track stops to let one melody sing — before it comes back as the "anthem".

## Where it comes from

Trance grew out of the **techno and EBM scene in Frankfurt, Germany, in the late 1980s and early 1990s**, and spread quickly across Europe (UK, Belgium, the Netherlands). Tracks usually sit between **125 and 150 BPM**, in 4/4, built from **16- and 32-beat phrases**. Instruments come in and out every 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars, and one central hook repeats through most of the track.

Three things set it apart from house and techno:

- **The kick is de-emphasised** to give space to the bassline.
- **Snare rolls** announce every transition and climax.
- **Rapid arpeggios**, mostly **minor keys**, and the **supersaw** — the waveform made famous by the Roland JP-8000.

**Hard trance** added the **gated pad** and the template every trance producer knows: **breakdown → build → anthem**.

## The family

| Style | What defines it |
|---|---|
| Progressive trance | smoother, chart-oriented; slow-moving layers |
| Uplifting trance | ~135–140 BPM, happier (major-leaning chords), long breakdowns, big drops, heavy sidechain |
| Psytrance / Goa | Goa (India) roots; a constant pounding bass; new layers every 4–8 bars; full-on ~140–148 |
| Hard / tech trance | faster and harder; tech trance mixes in techno |
| Vocal trance | pop song form on top; "soaring" vocals |

## The fingerprint

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tempo | 125–150 (uplifting ~138, psy ~145) |
| Kick | four on the floor, a bit quieter than in house — the bass gets the space |
| Bass | rolling 16ths **between** the kicks (`~ x x x` per beat) |
| Harmony | minor key, 4-chord loops, chords held for a whole bar |
| Arp | 16th-note arpeggio through the chord, often with a synced delay |
| Pad | supersaw chords chopped by a 16-step **trance gate** |
| Lead | supersaw hook with lots of reverb and delay |
| Arrangement | long; intro → groove → **long breakdown** → build → **anthem** → outro |

## 1. The rolling bass

The trance bassline is **three 16ths after every kick** — the kick takes the first 16th, the bass fills the other three. It sounds like one continuous rolling motion. A short filter envelope makes each note "pluck", and the first bass note after the kick is a bit quieter so the kick still punches through.

<Strudel title="rolling bass: kick + 3 bass 16ths" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.85)
  bass: n("<0 -2 -1 -3>").scale("A1:minor").struct("[~ x x x]*4")
    .s("sawtooth").lpf(350).lpenv(3).lpdecay(0.07)
    .decay(0.1).sustain(0).gain("[0 0.5 0.7 0.7]*4")
  hats: s("[~ oh]*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.35)
`} />

<Callout kind="tip">
`.decay(0.1).sustain(0)` keeps every note shorter than a 16th (≈ 109 ms at 138 BPM), so the notes never overlap and the roll stays tight. Longer notes smear into a drone.
</Callout>

## 2. The arp

A 16th-note pattern walks up and down the chord tones. Because it is added to the progression **before** `.scale()` (lesson 8), it follows the chords automatically. A dotted-8th delay (`3/16` of a bar) fills the gaps with echoes that land on the off-16ths.

The progression is written as `"<0 -2 -1 -3>"`: the same Am – F – G – Em as `"<0 5 6 4>"`, but each chord sits **below** A instead of above it. The parts move by small steps, so the arp stays in one range instead of jumping up an octave on every chord.

<Strudel title="16th-note arp following the chords" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  arp: n("[0 2 4 2 7 4 2 4]*2").add(n("<0 -2 -1 -3>")).scale("A4:minor")
    .s("supersaw").unison(3).detune(0.2)
    .lpf(sine.range(1200, 4500).slow(16)).decay(0.12).sustain(0)
    .delay(0.35).delaysync(3/16).delayfeedback(0.4).gain(0.35)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.85)
`} />

## 3. The trance gate

A **trance gate** chops a held chord on a 16-step rhythm. In a DAW it is a gate plugin or an LFO on the volume; in Strudel it is simpler — play the chord as short 16th notes and choose which steps play with `struct()`. A very short release makes the cuts sharp.

<Strudel title="gated supersaw pad (16-step gate)" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  gate: n("[0,2,4,7]").add(n("<0 -2 -1 -3>")).scale("A3:minor")
    .struct("x x ~ x x ~ x ~ x x ~ x x ~ x x")
    .s("supersaw").unison(5).detune(0.3).lpf(3000)
    .attack(0.005).sustain(1).release(0.03).gain(0.3)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.85)
`} />

<TryThis>
Change the gate pattern — every `~` is a hole. Try `"x*16"` (no gate at all), then `"[x ~]*8"` (straight 8ths). The chords stay the same; only the rhythm of the cuts changes the feel.
</TryThis>

## 4. Breakdown → build → anthem

This is the heart of trance. The **breakdown** removes the kick and bass and leaves the hook alone over a pad, with big reverb — often for 32 bars. The **build** brings in a snare roll and opens the filter. Then the **anthem**: the same hook, now over everything.

<Strudel title="the hook alone (breakdown sound)" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  pad: n("[0,2,4,7]").add(n("<0 -2 -1 -3>")).scale("A3:minor")
    .s("supersaw").attack(1).release(2).sustain(1).lpf(1800).gain(0.2).room(0.6)
  lead: n("<[4 ~ 4 ~ 2 ~ 4 ~ 7 ~ ~ 6 ~ 4 ~ ~] [5 ~ 5 ~ 4 ~ 5 ~ 7 ~ ~ 9 ~ 7 ~ ~] [6 ~ 6 ~ 4 ~ 6 ~ 8 ~ ~ 9 ~ 8 ~ ~] [4 ~ 4 ~ 2 ~ 4 ~ 6 ~ ~ 4 ~ 1 ~ ~]>")
    .scale("A4:minor").s("supersaw").unison(7).detune(0.3).lpf(5000)
    .attack(0.005).decay(0.3).sustain(0.4).release(0.2)
    .room(0.7).roomsize(6).delay(0.3).delaysync(3/16).gain(0.35)
`} />

<Callout kind="info">
Uplifting trance leans on **sidechain** ("ducking the kick") so the big pads and leads pump around the kick. In the template below everything melodic sits on orbit 2 and the kick ducks it — the same tool as in lesson 15.
</Callout>

## 5. A taste of psytrance

Psytrance keeps the rolling bass but plays it on **one note** for long stretches, faster (~145), with a darker, squelchy lead and new layers every 4–8 bars.

<Strudel title="psytrance: one-note roll + squelchy lead" code={`
  setcpm(145/4)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.95)
  bass: note("e1").struct("[~ x x x]*4").s("sawtooth")
    .lpf(300).lpenv(3).lpdecay(0.05).decay(0.08).sustain(0).gain(0.75)
  lead: n("0 ~ 1 0 ~ 3 ~ 1 0 ~ 7 ~ 6 ~ 3 1").scale("E3:phrygian")
    .s("sawtooth").lpf(sine.range(400, 2500).slow(8)).lpq(12)
    .decay(0.1).sustain(0).delay(0.3).delaysync(3/16).gain(0.35)
  hats: s("hh*16").bank("RolandTR909").gain("[0.15 0.3]*8")
`} />

## The template

A 136-bar track (≈ 4 min at 138 BPM). For a 7-minute club version, double the intro, groove and outro.

<Strudel title="trance track template" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  const prog = "<0 -2 -1 -3>"
  const kick0 = s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.85)   // plain kick: nothing on orbit 2 to duck yet
  const kick  = kick0.duckorbit(2).duckdepth(0.6).duckattack(0.2)
  const hats  = s("[~ oh]*4, hh*16").bank("RolandTR909").gain("[0.15 0.25 0.4 0.25]*4")
  const clap  = s("~ cp ~ cp").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.55).room(0.3)
  const bass  = n(prog).scale("A1:minor").struct("[~ x x x]*4")
    .s("sawtooth").lpf(350).lpenv(3).lpdecay(0.07)
    .decay(0.1).sustain(0).gain("[0 0.5 0.7 0.7]*4")
  const arp   = n("[0 2 4 2 7 4 2 4]*2").add(n(prog)).scale("A4:minor")
    .s("supersaw").unison(3).detune(0.2).lpf(3500).decay(0.12).sustain(0)
    .delay(0.3).delaysync(3/16).gain(0.25).orbit(2)
  const gate  = n("[0,2,4,7]").add(n(prog)).scale("A3:minor")
    .struct("x x ~ x x ~ x ~ x x ~ x x ~ x x")
    .s("supersaw").unison(5).detune(0.3).lpf(3000)
    .attack(0.005).sustain(1).release(0.03).gain(0.25).orbit(2)
  const pad   = n("[0,2,4,7]").add(n(prog)).scale("A3:minor")
    .s("supersaw").attack(1).release(2).sustain(1).lpf(1800).gain(0.2).room(0.6).orbit(2)
  const lead  = n("<[4 ~ 4 ~ 2 ~ 4 ~ 7 ~ ~ 6 ~ 4 ~ ~] [5 ~ 5 ~ 4 ~ 5 ~ 7 ~ ~ 9 ~ 7 ~ ~] [6 ~ 6 ~ 4 ~ 6 ~ 8 ~ ~ 9 ~ 8 ~ ~] [4 ~ 4 ~ 2 ~ 4 ~ 6 ~ ~ 4 ~ 1 ~ ~]>")
    .scale("A4:minor").s("supersaw").unison(7).detune(0.3).lpf(5000)
    .attack(0.005).decay(0.3).sustain(0.4).release(0.2)
    .room(0.5).delay(0.3).delaysync(3/16).gain(0.32).orbit(2)
  const build = stack(
    s("sd*<4 4 8 8 16 16 32 32>").bank("RolandTR909")
      .gain(saw.range(0.3, 1).slow(8)),
    s("white*16").decay(0.07).sustain(0)
      .hpf(saw.range(300, 12000).slow(8)).gain(saw.range(0.05, 0.35).slow(8)),
    lead.lpf(saw.range(600, 5000).slow(8)), pad
  ).mask("<1!7 [1 1 1 0]>")

  arrange(
    [16, stack(kick0, hats)],                                  // intro
    [16, stack(kick, hats, clap, bass, arp.lpf(1200))],       // groove: bass + filtered arp
    [16, stack(kick, hats, clap, bass, arp, gate)],           // groove: full arp + gate
    [32, stack(pad, lead.room(0.8), arp.gain(0.12))],         // breakdown: the hook alone
    [8,  build],                                              // build-up
    [32, stack(kick, hats, clap, bass, arp, gate, lead)],     // anthem
    [16, stack(kick0, hats, bass)]                             // outro
  )
`} />
