**The rule that saves tracks:** do the steps **in order** and give each one a time limit. A good loop in 30 minutes, an arrangement in the next hour. Polishing a loop for hours is the #1 reason tracks never get finished.

## Step 1–4: the loop (≈ 30 min)

Start from this skeleton. Every part is a labelled line, so you can mute (`_`) and unmute while you work. The `slider()` calls draw real knobs in the editor — drag them while it plays.

<Strudel title="starting skeleton with live knobs" code={`
  setcpm(128/4)
  const key  = 'A:minor'
  const prog = "<0 0 5 4>"

  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.95)
  hats: s("[~ oh]*4, hh*16").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.35)
  clap: s("~ cp ~ cp").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.7)
  bass: n(prog).scale("A1:minor").struct("[~ x]*4")
    .s("sawtooth").lpf(slider(450, 150, 1500))
    .decay(0.15).sustain(0.3).gain(0.8)
  _chords: n("[0,2,4,6]").add(n(prog)).scale(key).struct("[~ x ~ ~]*4")
    .s("gm_drawbar_organ").decay(0.2).sustain(0).hpf(200).gain(0.55)
  _hook: n("0 ~ ~ 2 ~ ~ 4 ~ ~ ~ 3 ~ 2 ~ ~ ~").scale("A4:minor")
    .s("square").lpf(slider(2200, 500, 6000)).decay(0.15).sustain(0.2).gain(0.45)
`} />

<TryThis>
Press Play. Drag the bass filter knob. Remove `_` from `chords`, then from `hook`. Change the progression to `"<0 3 5 4>"`. You now have a loop you made decisions in — that is step 4 done.
</TryThis>

## Step 5: the sound pass (≈ 20 min)

Go part by part, solo it (mute the rest with `_`), and ask one question: **does it sound like the genre?** Use lessons 10–12. Do not touch the arrangement yet.

## Step 6: arrange (≈ 45 min)

Turn the labels into `const` layers and list sections with `arrange()` (lessons 13–14). Start from the genre template (lessons 16–23) and only change what you need.

<Callout kind="tip">
Shortcut: write the sections first with only `kick` in each — `[16, kick], [16, kick], …` — and check the total length feels right. Then fill in the layers.
</Callout>

## Step 7: mix (≈ 20 min)

Run the mix checklist from lesson 15. Fix the biggest problem first (usually: kick vs bass, or something too loud).

## Step 8: finish

A track is finished when the checklist passes, not when it is perfect.

| ✓ | Check |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Tempo and key decided and consistent |
| ☐ | Intro and outro are DJ-friendly (drums first) |
| ☐ | Every change lands on an 8-bar boundary |
| ☐ | At least one breakdown and one real drop |
| ☐ | Something changes every 8 bars; small variation inside bars |
| ☐ | Kick loudest, nothing clips, low end mono |
| ☐ | You listened at low volume and on a second system |
| ☐ | You took a break and listened once more with fresh ears |

## The 10 rules of this course

1. `setcpm(BPM/4)`: one cycle = one bar.
2. One key per track, everything through `.scale(key)`.
3. Kick on every beat, clap on 2 and 4, open hat on the offbeat.
4. Bass between the kicks, on chord roots.
5. Octaves and fifths in the low end, thirds only in the mids.
6. Start sounds from a saw and shape them with the filter.
7. Change something every 8 bars; fill in the last bar.
8. Tension before release: roll + riser + a silent beat.
9. Kick loudest; high-pass everything else; low end mono.
10. Finish, then start the next track.
