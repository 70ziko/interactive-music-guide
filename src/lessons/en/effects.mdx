Synthesis makes the sound. Effects put it **in a space**, **in time**, and give it **attitude**. Used well they glue a track together; used everywhere they turn it into soup.

## How effects are wired in Strudel: orbits

Every pattern plays through an **orbit** — an effects bus. **Reverb and delay live on the orbit**: all patterns on orbit 1 share one reverb and one delay line. *How much* of a note goes in (`room`, `delay`) is set per note, but the reverb size and the delay time/feedback are shared, and the most recent settings win. Filters, distortion and envelopes are per note.

  { name: 'Orbit (bus)', desc: 'shared reverb + delay for every pattern on it', code: '.orbit(2)' },
  { name: 'Output', desc: 'all orbits summed to your speakers', code: '' },
]} />

**Practical rule:** give parts that need *different* reverb or delay their own orbit. Keep kick and bass on an orbit with no reverb.

<Strudel title="separate spaces with orbits" code={`
  setcpm(124/4)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909")                       // orbit 1, dry
  stabs: chord("<Am7 Dm7>").voicing().struct("[~ x]*4")
    .s("gm_drawbar_organ").decay(0.2).sustain(0)
    .room(0.6).roomsize(4).orbit(2)                        // big room
  lead: n("0 ~ 2 4 ~ 2 ~ ~").scale("A4:minor").s("square")
    .lpf(2000).decay(0.15).sustain(0.2).gain(0.4)
    .delay(0.4).delaysync(3/16).delayfeedback(0.45).orbit(3) // echoes
`} />

## Reverb: space

| Control | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `.room(0..1)` | how much of the sound is sent to the reverb (wet amount) |
| `.roomsize(0..10)` | size / length of the space |

Short, small reverb = a room; long, big reverb = a hall or a breakdown "wash". Reverb pushes a sound **back** in the mix: dry = close, wet = far.

<Strudel title="dry → small room → huge hall" code={`
  setcpm(120/4)
  s("~ cp ~ cp").bank("RolandTR909")
    .room("<0 0.3 0.8>").roomsize("<0 1.5 8>")
`} />

## Delay: echoes in time

| Control | What it sets |
|---|---|
| `.delay(0..1)` | wet amount |
| `.delaysync(x)` | delay time in **bars** (cycles) — always in tempo |
| `.delaytime(s)` | delay time in **seconds** |
| `.delayfeedback(0..1)` | how many repeats (keep under ~0.7) |

Echoes that land on the grid sound musical; echoes off the grid sound messy. `delaysync` does the maths for you. The classic dance-music values are **1/8**, **3/16** (dotted 8th) and **1/4**.

## Distortion: harmonics and attitude

Distortion bends the waveform, which **adds harmonics** (lesson 1). More harmonics = brighter, louder-sounding, more aggressive. Hard house and bounce kicks, basses and hoovers are almost always distorted to some degree.

| Control | Character |
|---|---|
| `.shape(0..1)` | soft saturation — warmth |
| `.distort(0..∞)` | harder clipping — grit |
| `.crush(1..16)` | bit reduction — lo-fi, digital |
| `.coarse(n)` | sample-rate reduction — aliasing, metallic |

<Strudel title="the same bass: clean, shape, distort, crush" code={`
  setcpm(140/4)
  note("[~ a1]*4").s("sawtooth").lpf(600)
    .decay(0.15).sustain(0.3)
    .shape("<0 0.6 0 0>").distort("<0 0 2 0>").crush("<16 16 16 5>")
    ._scope()
`} />

## Filters as effects

Besides shaping a synth, filters are **performance tools**:

- `.hpf()` sweep up = thin the sound out before a drop,
- `.lpf()` sweep up = "open up" a muffled loop,
- `.djf(x)` = a DJ-mixer filter on the **whole orbit**: below 0.5 is low-pass, above 0.5 is high-pass, 0.5 is off.

<Strudel title="DJ filter on a whole groove" code={`
  setcpm(126/4)
  stack(
    s("bd*4, ~ cp ~ cp, [~ oh]*4, hh*16").bank("RolandTR909"),
    note("[~ a1]*4").s("sawtooth").lpf(500).decay(0.15).sustain(0.3)
  ).djf("<0.5 0.25 0.5 0.8>")   // off, muffled, off, thin
`} />

## Compression and ducking

A **compressor** turns loud moments down so the overall level is more even. `compressor("threshold:ratio:knee:attack:release")` — threshold in dB, ratio like 4 (4:1).

**Ducking** (sidechain) is compression triggered by *another* sound: the kick turns the bass or pads down for a moment. It is the main source of the "pumping" feel. You met `duckorbit` in lesson 9; here is what it does to the volume:
