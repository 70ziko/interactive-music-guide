Acid techno takes the squelching bassline of Chicago acid house and drops it into **harder, faster techno**. One machine defines it: the Roland **TB-303**.

## Where it comes from

Acid techno grew out of acid house in **Europe and the US in the late 1980s to early 1990s**, with a famous **London** underground scene around crews like the Liberators. The "acid" is the harsh, squelching sound of the 303, made by turning the **resonance up** and the **cutoff down**, then moving them. Compared with acid house, it applies that sound to harder-edged techno. Tempos are usually fast — roughly **130–150 BPM**.

## The fingerprint

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tempo | ~130–150 |
| Kick | hard 909 kick, often distorted, four on the floor |
| Hats | offbeat open hat, driving 16ths |
| **303 line** | 16th-note bassline, 1–2 bars, resonance high, cutoff and accents moving |
| Extra | sometimes a second 303, claps on 2 & 4, very little else |
| Arrangement | the 303 **is** the arrangement: the track evolves by riding its knobs |

## Anatomy of a 303 line

A 303 pattern has four ingredients. Strudel can do three directly and approximate the fourth:

| Ingredient | What it does | Strudel |
|---|---|---|
| Notes | mostly root, octave, a few scale notes | `note("a1 a2 c2 …")` |
| Rests / ties | gaps make the groove | `~` and `@2` |
| **Accent** | some notes louder and brighter | patterned `gain` + `lpenv` |
| Slide | glide between notes | no glide on the standard synths (`slide` only affects Strudel's ZZFX sounds) — use octave jumps and short decays instead |

<Strudel title="plain line → with accents (alternating 2 bars)" code={`
  setcpm(136/4)
  note("a1 a1 a2 a1 c2 a1 [a1 a2] e2 a1 a1 g2 a1 c2 a2 a1 e2")
    .s("sawtooth").ftype("ladder")
    .lpf(500).lpq(16)
    // bars 1–2: no accents · bars 3–4: accents on steps 3, 7, 11, 14
    .lpenv("<1!2 [1 1 5 1 1 1 5 1 1 1 5 1 1 5 1 1]!2>")
    .gain("<0.6!2 [.55 .55 .8 .55 .55 .55 .8 .55 .55 .55 .8 .55 .55 .8 .55 .55]!2>")
    .lpdecay(0.1).decay(0.15).sustain(0.2)
    .distort(0.7)
    ._pianoroll()
`} />

## Riding the knobs

The 303 sound comes alive when **cutoff and resonance move**. In a live set you ride them by hand — in Strudel use `slider()` (drag the knobs that appear) or slow signals.

<Strudel title="ride cutoff and resonance live" code={`
  setcpm(138/4)
  acid: note("a1 [a1 a2] c2 a1 [~ g2] a1 e2 [a1 c3]")
    .s("sawtooth").ftype("ladder")
    .lpf(slider(600, 150, 3000))
    .lpq(slider(14, 1, 25))
    .lpenv(slider(3, 0, 6)).lpdecay(0.1)
    .decay(0.15).sustain(0.2).distort(slider(0.8, 0, 3)).gain(0.55)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").distort(1).gain(0.9)
  hats: s("[~ oh]*4, hh*16").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.35)
`} />

<TryThis>
Press Play. Keep the cutoff knob low (~300) and slowly raise the resonance. Then sweep the cutoff up over ~8 bars. That one gesture is most of acid techno.
</TryThis>

## Distortion and the kick

Distorting the 303 makes it louder and harsher — but a distorted 303 also fills the low end and can **mask the kick**. Keep the 303's low end in check (`hpf` around 60–80 Hz) or duck it with the kick.

<Strudel title="distorted 303 that leaves room for the kick" code={`
  setcpm(140/4)
  acid: note("a1 a1 a2 a1 c2 a1 [a1 a2] e2")
    .s("sawtooth").ftype("ladder").lpf(sine.range(400, 2500).slow(8)).lpq(15)
    .lpenv(3).lpdecay(0.1).decay(0.15).sustain(0.2)
    .distort(2).hpf(70).gain(0.45).orbit(2)
  kick: s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").distort(1.2).gain(0.95)
    .duckorbit(2).duckdepth(0.6).duckattack(0.12)
`} />

## The template

A 144-bar acid techno track (≈ 4.1 min at 140 BPM). The 303 changes character every section through cutoff, resonance and distortion.

<Strudel title="acid techno track template" code={`
  setcpm(140/4)
  const kick = s("bd*4").bank("RolandTR909").distort(1.2).gain(0.95)
    .duckorbit(2).duckdepth(0.55).duckattack(0.12)
  const hats = s("[~ oh]*4, hh*16").bank("RolandTR909").gain("[0.25 0.35 0.55 0.35]*4")
  const clap = s("~ cp ~ cp").bank("RolandTR909").gain(0.55)
  const line = note("a1 a1 a2 a1 c2 a1 [a1 a2] e2 a1 a1 g2 a1 c2 a2 a1 e2")
    .s("sawtooth").ftype("ladder")
    .lpenv(3).lpdecay(0.1).decay(0.15).sustain(0.2).hpf(70).orbit(2)
  const roll = s("sd*<4 4 8 8 16 16 32 32>").bank("RolandTR909")
    .gain(saw.range(0.3, 0.9).slow(8))

  arrange(
    [16, stack(kick, hats)],                                                   // intro
    [32, stack(kick, hats, line.lpf(saw.range(250, 900).slow(32)).lpq(10).gain(0.45))],  // acid creeps in
    [32, stack(kick, hats, clap, line.lpf(sine.range(500, 2500).slow(16)).lpq(16).distort(1).gain(0.45))], // full squelch
    [8,  stack(line.lpf(saw.range(300, 3500).slow(8)).lpq(20).distort(2).gain(0.4), roll)], // break: acid alone, roll
    [32, stack(kick, hats, clap, line.lpf(sine.range(800, 3500).slow(8)).lpq(18).distort(2.5).gain(0.42))], // peak
    [24, stack(kick, hats, line.lpf(saw.range(1500, 250).slow(24)).lpq(10).gain(0.45))]  // outro: acid closes
  )
`} />
