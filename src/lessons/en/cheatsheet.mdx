## Time (3)

<Formula>setcpm(BPM / 4)   →   1 cycle = 1 bar = 4 beats = 16 steps</Formula>

| Value | Formula | At 128 BPM |
|---|---|---|
| 1 beat | 60 000 / BPM ms | 468.8 ms |
| 1 bar | 4 × beat | 1.875 s |
| 1/16 step | beat / 4 | 117.2 ms |
| 8 bars | 32 × beat | 15 s |
| `late(x)` | x × bar | `late(0.01)` = 18.75 ms |

## Mini-notation (3)

| Write | Means |
|---|---|
| `a b c d` | 4 equal steps in one cycle |
| `~` | rest |
| `[a b]` | squeeze into one step |
| `a*4` / `a!4` | repeat inside the step / copy as 4 steps |
| `<a b>` | one per cycle |
| `a, b` | together (layers) |
| `a(3,8)` | Euclid: 3 hits over 8 |
| `a?` | 50 % chance |
| `a@3` | 3 steps long |
| `sd*<4 8 16>` | patterned repeat count |

## Pitch (1, 2)

<Formula>f = 440 · 2^((m − 69) / 12)      cents = 1200 · log2(ratio)</Formula>

| Interval | Semitones | Ratio | Scale steps (7-note) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Octave | 12 | 2 : 1 | 7 |
| Fifth | 7 | 3 : 2 | 4 |
| Fourth | 5 | 4 : 3 | 3 |
| Major / minor 3rd | 4 / 3 | 5 : 4 / 6 : 5 | 2 |

**Order matters:** `n(…).add(n(…)).scale(key)` — do arithmetic on `n` **before** `.scale()`. After `.scale()` it is silently ignored.

## Keys and chords in A minor (6, 7)

| Degree `n` | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note | A | B | C | D | E | F | G |
| Triad | Am (i) | B° (ii°) | C (III) | Dm (iv) | Em (v) | F (VI) | G (VII) |

| Progression | `n` pattern | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| i – VI – III – VII | `"<0 5 2 6>"` | anthemic |
| i – i – VI – v | `"<0 0 5 4>"` | house classic |
| i – VI – iv – v | `"<0 5 3 4>"` | bounce |
| i – iv | `"<0 3>"` | hypnotic vamp |

## Functions by job

| Job | Functions |
|---|---|
| Sound | `s()` `note()` `n().scale()` `chord().voicing()` `bank()` |
| Rhythm | `struct()` `mask()` `fast()` `slow()` `swingBy()` `late()` |
| Variation | `<…>` `lastOf()` `firstOf()` `sometimesBy()` `degradeBy()` `off()` `rev()` `ply()` |
| Envelope | `attack()` `decay()` `sustain()` `release()` `penv()` `pdecay()` |
| Filter | `lpf()` `lpq()` `hpf()` `bpf()` `lpenv()` `lpattack()` `ftype()` `djf()` |
| Effects | `room()` `roomsize()` `delay()` `delaysync()` `delayfeedback()` `distort()` `shape()` `crush()` `phaser()` |
| Mix | `gain()` `pan()` `orbit()` `duckorbit()` `duckdepth()` `duckattack()` `compressor()` |
| Movement | `sine` `saw` `perlin` + `.range(a,b)` `.slow(n)` |
| Structure | labels `kick:` · mute `_kick:` · `stack()` · `arrange([bars, pat], …)` |

## Genres (16–18)

| | House | Bounce / donk | Hard house |
|---|---|---|---|
| BPM | 115–130 (mostly 120–128) | ~140–150 | 135–165 (~150) |
| Offbeat | open hat | donk | bass stabs |
| Kick | clean | punchy, driven | hard, distorted |
| Signature | m7 stabs, piano, acid | sing-along hook | hoover, long string, drum-roll drop |
